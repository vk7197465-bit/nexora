Common Questions

Quick answers to common dashboard questions.

How do I request installation?

Open Setup Requests, select a service and location, and submit your preferred details.

Is my appointment confirmed?

No. A saved appointment is a preference, not confirmation from an installation team.

How do I register a warranty?

Open Warranties and enter the product name, brand, serial number and purchase date.

Will dark mode apply everywhere?

This dashboard saves your theme preference in localStorage. The public pages must read the same setting to synchronize it.
