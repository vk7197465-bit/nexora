Still have a question? Contact us ↗
What is included in home automation?

Depending on your needs, a smart-home setup can include connected lighting, curtains, voice control, security devices, and centralised control.

Can I automate my existing home?

Many existing homes can be upgraded. The right approach depends on your wiring, devices, network, and the automation features you want.

How much does a smart-home setup cost?

Pricing depends on the number of devices, selected features, installation requirements, and property size. Contact us for a solution based on your needs.

Can I control devices from my phone?

Compatible devices can often be controlled through a smartphone app. Available features depend on the selected devices and their supported platforms.

Do you provide installation assistance?

Contact our team to discuss installation, configuration, and support options available for your location and chosen solution.
